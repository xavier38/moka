☕ What's new in Moka 1.0.12

  • Extending an unlimited session (+30 min, +1 h) no longer shortens it, and extending a scheduled session now really lasts.
  • The schedule also works on the day the clocks change, and desktop Macs are no longer kept awake forever by the “external display” option.
  • Safer and lighter: moka:// links are checked, and Moka wakes up far less often when idle.

Earlier versions

Moka 1.0.11

  • When you choose to remove Moka from the Dock, its icon no longer comes back by itself: only the little cup stays at the top of the screen.

Moka 1.0.10

  • The app and widget are easier to use with VoiceOver: switches and actions now announce their purpose, and widget buttons are larger.

Moka 1.0.9

  • Moka now tells you when the battery or Low Power Mode prevents it from starting. The warning also appears when you use the widget.
