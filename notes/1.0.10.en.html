☕ What's new in Moka 1.0.10

  • The app and widget are easier to use with VoiceOver: switches and actions now announce their purpose, and widget buttons are larger.

Earlier versions

Moka 1.0.9

  • Moka now tells you when the battery or Low Power Mode prevents it from starting. The warning also appears when you use the widget.

Moka 1.0.8

  • Fixed a crash when Moka started with the battery below the selected stop limit.

Moka 1.0.7

  • New “Dock and menu bar” section at the top of Settings → General: one switch to remove Moka from the Dock and keep only the little cup at the top of the screen.
