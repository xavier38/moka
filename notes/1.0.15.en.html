☕ What's new in Moka 1.0.15

  • Text can now be enlarged to 200%. Contrast, control labels and support for the Reduce Motion setting have been improved.

Earlier versions

Moka 1.0.14

  • When a new version of Moka is ready, it now shows at the very top of the cup menu. One click and Moka offers to install it right away.

Moka 1.0.13

  • Anonymous statistics are now on by default to help improve Moka. You can turn them off in Settings › General. Never your name, your email or what you do on your Mac.

Moka 1.0.12

  • Extending an unlimited session (+30 min, +1 h) no longer shortens it, and extending a scheduled session now really lasts.
  • The schedule also works on the day the clocks change, and desktop Macs are no longer kept awake forever by the “external display” option.
  • Safer and lighter: moka:// links are checked, and Moka wakes up far less often when idle.
